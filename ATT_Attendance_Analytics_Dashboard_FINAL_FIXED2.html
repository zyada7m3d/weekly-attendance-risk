    KNOWN GAPS

    What this demo does not decide

    • No causal diagnosis: the dashboard shows patterns, not reasons for absence.
    • No intervention workflow: alerts are analytical, not a case-management system.
    • No predictive model: risk flags are deterministic and rule-based.
    • No policy override: institutional attendance policies should be applied separately.
